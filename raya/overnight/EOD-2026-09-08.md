## Four things that need your decision

**1. The location sentence's caller-place slot.** Not prose-fixable — it carries two instructions
that cannot both be obeyed ("this is a literal substituted token, nothing to resolve", added because
six calls resolved it to the stale profile city; and "convert it first — drop the digits, write it in
Devanagari", added this morning). Obeying the first leaks the raw value; attempting the second makes
the model reach for a place it already knows. **The same failure occurs at 69k and at 219k in the
same proportions**, so shrinking the prompt cannot fix it.
- *Preferred:* upstream sends `location_spoken`, already digit-free and already in the call's script,
  and the prompt reads a token — the one thing it does reliably. (`ESCALATION-data-team.md` §5.)
- *Prompt-only fallback:* drop the caller-place slot and speak only the jobs' cities, which come off
  array fields and have never been misread. Costs the area confirmation.

**2. The ordinal running count.** Fails on **58% of the calls that reach a second batch** (14 of 24
over 1,675 cached calls). The rule is unambiguous; it asks the model to carry a counter across
unbounded turns. Removing that state changes spoken output, hence your call:
- drop the numbering on later batches ("एक और जॉब है — …") and let selection happen by role and
  company, which the phonetic-confirmation rules already cover; or
- make the numbering explicitly per-batch and re-anchor selection on the name.

**3. How small the prompt should actually be.** The rewrite is at **68,744 chars from 219,519 (69%
smaller)** with every spoken line and tool contract intact, running on its own bot. 50,000 is not
reachable without removing behaviour — deleting both worked calls, the whole ASR section, all
Situations *and* the canonical spellings together still leaves 56,657, and those are the samples the
model actually follows, the phonetic guards, the dignity handling and the TTS spellings. The menu
with its arithmetic is in `raya/slim/README.md`.

  **And the premise did not survive contact with measurement.** Ten A/B calls, five per side, same
  fixture and persona: fat median **16.00** s per agent turn, slim **14.75**. A 69% cut bought about
  7%. Meanwhile `hold_message` is never played — **348 of 348** tool calls across 1,152 calls on all
  18 bots sat in silence for the whole round-trip, 189 of them `get_profile`, which fires at the top
  of every call. That is where the latency is (`ESCALATION-litwiz.md` §3).

**4. Whether to keep the slim bot.** It is `140d13ca`, carries no production traffic, and is a
field-by-field clone of `115b38a5` apart from the prompt. On the A/B both prompts failed on the same
classes at similar rates — payload names in Latin, the ordinal restart, the location substitution —
so the extra 150k is not buying adherence on anything the harness can see. It is also not the cause.
